# meeting-notes

Turn a transcript into decisions, action items and open questions.

## Output
1. **Decisions:** what was agreed, one line each.
2. **Action items:** who, what, by when. If the transcript gives no owner or date, write "owner: unassigned" or "date: not set". Do not guess.
3. **Open questions:** things raised but not settled.
4. **Context (optional):** two sentences on what the meeting was about.

## Steps
1. Read the whole transcript before writing.
2. Collect statements with a decision or a commitment ("we will", "I'll take it", "let's go with").
3. Write the lists in the order above. Keep each item to one line.

## Rules
- Use the speakers' names exactly as they appear.
- Do not add action items nobody committed to.
