Club Pliego — free for Collected readers this month

Pliego Editions is offering Collected readers a free 30-day Club Pliego trial through the partner program. Use the panel on the right to activate your free membership.

C
By the Collected team · 2 min read

Pliego Editions has built one of the most consistent digital print catalogs over the last five years — 847 pieces across geometric, abstract, and editorial series. Club Pliego is their membership program: full catalog access, unlimited downloads, and new releases each month.

Through November, Collected readers can activate a free 30-day trial. Fill in the form on the right with your name, phone, and email, then click Get free access. Your account confirmation and membership terms arrive by email within a few minutes.

What's included in Club Pliego

  • 847 prints · Full resolution PNG · Sorted by series and year
  • New editions each month before the public store
  • Unlimited re-downloads at any time
  • Partner program offers through Collected and other publications

After the free month, Club Pliego is $6/month. Cancel any time from your account settings. This partner offer is available through November only.
